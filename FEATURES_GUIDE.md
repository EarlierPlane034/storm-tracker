## 📡 Main Features by Category

### 🗺️ RADAR & MAP (Core)
**Tab: Radar (default)**
- Real-time NEXRAD reflectivity (dBZ)
- Velocity scans for rotation detection
- Product selector: CREF, N0Q, N0U, N0S, SVIS, SIR
- Animation controls with frame-by-frame scrubbing
- Map layers: Outlook, observations, tornado history
- GPS location tracking

**How to Use:**
1. Select a radar product from the left rail
2. Use play/pause to animate radar loops
3. Tap any storm cell on the map for details
4. Use the + button to zoom in on areas of interest

---

### ⛈️ STORM ANALYSIS (Week 1)
**Tab: Storms**
- List of all detected storm cells
- AI severity score (0-100)
- Storm type classification
- Distance and ETA from your location
- Sort by severity or distance

**Tab: Alerts**
- Active NWS tornado warnings
- Severe thunderstorm warnings
- Winter storm alerts
- Flash flood warnings
- Proximity filtering by distance

---

### 📊 ADVANCED ANALYSIS (Week 2)
**Tab: Analysis** ← NEW!

**6 Analysis Subtabs:**

1. **Overview** - Quick snapshot of selected storm
2. **Hodograph** - Wind shear profile & SRH calculation
3. **Environment** - CAPE, CIN, LCL, freezing level heatmaps
4. **Trends** - Storm lifecycle and metric tracking
5. **Rankings** - Compare multiple storms
6. **Performance** - Render optimization & mobile metrics

**How to Use:**
1. Tap any storm on the map to select it
2. Open Analysis tab to see detailed breakdown
3. Check Hodograph for wind shear environment
4. Review Trends to see storm intensification rate
5. Use Rankings to compare 2-3 storms side-by-side

### 🔥 WEEK 3 ADVANCED FEATURES (NEW!)
**Tab: Week 3** ← ALL 8 NEW FEATURES!

#### 1. 🤖 ML Predictions
- **Hail Probability** - AI-predicted likelihood of hail
- **Tornado Genesis Risk** - Tornado development probability
- **Storm Type** - Supercell, QLCS, multicell classification
- **Longevity** - Expected storm lifetime in minutes
- **Rapid Intensification** - Detection of sudden growth
- **Storm Similarity** - Compare to historical storms

**When to Use:** After selecting a storm to assess threats

#### 2. 👥 Community & Social
- **Spotter Reports** - Verified storm reports from users
- **Chaser Leaderboard** - Top performers by intercepts
- **Active Storm Tracking** - Follow storms with other chasers
- **Report Verification** - Community-validated sightings

**When to Use:** To see real reports and connect with chasers

#### 3. 🔮 Advanced Forecasting
- **Hail Swath** - Predicted path of hail over 60 minutes
- **Tornado Touchdown Zone** - Most likely touchdown location
- **Supercell Splitting** - Will storm split? When? Where?
- **Echo Top Trends** - Storm height forecast

**When to Use:** Planning intercept routes and safety

#### 4. 🎙️ Voice & Audio
- **Voice Alerts** - Speak warnings for dangerous storms
- **Warning Tones** - Siren sounds for immediate attention
- **Podcast Briefing** - Narrative summary of all storms
- **Voice Commands** - Ask questions: "Show nearest storm"

**When to Use:** Driving or hands-free operation

#### 5. 🛡️ Chase Safety (Critical!)
- **Route Planning** - Calculate safest intercept path
- **Safe Havens** - Find nearest shelters (buildings, garages)
- **Lightning Alerts** - Real-time strike proximity warnings
- **Go/No-Go Score** - 0-100 recommendation to chase or stay safe
  - 75+: GO - Excellent opportunity
  - 60-74: GOOD - Chase if prepared
  - 40-59: MARGINAL - Probably not worth it
  - <40: NO-GO - Too risky

**IMPORTANT:** Always follow NWS warnings and use your judgment!

**When to Use:** Before and during every chase

#### 6. 💾 Database & History
- **Accuracy Metrics** - How accurate your predictions are
- **Seasonal Statistics** - Tornado/hail frequency by month
- **Chase Sessions** - Past chase day reports
- **Data Export** - Download as CSV or JSON
- **Storm Replay** - Rewatch storm evolution

**When to Use:** End of season review or skill assessment

#### 7. 📈 Charts & 3D
- **Storm Structure** - 2D cross-section view (N-S or E-W)
- **3D Rotation** - 3D cube showing storm geometry
- **Multi-Storm Comparison** - Bar charts comparing threats
- **Forecast Graphs** - Reflectivity trends over 30 min

**When to Use:** Understanding storm structure and evolution

#### 8. 🎓 Education & Training
- **Radar Pattern Lessons** - 4 structured lessons
  - Supercell identification
  - TVS detection
  - Hail probability
  - Derecho patterns
- **Storm Quizzes** - Multiple choice assessments
- **Spotter Certification** - Path to official certification
- **Identification Game** - Interactive scenarios with scoring

**When to Use:** Learn radar interpretation and build skills

## 🎯 Navigation Tips

### Bottom Tab Bar
Shows 9 main sections:
```
Radar | Storms | Alerts | Reports | Analysis | Week 3 | AI | Features | Settings
```

**Swipe left** to see all tabs (they scroll if screen is narrow)

### When Viewing a Storm
1. **Map view** - See storm circle and environment
2. **Storm sheet** - Tap circle → detailed analysis pops up
3. **Analysis tab** - Deep dive with charts and trends
4. **Week 3 tab** - Advanced ML predictions and forecasting
5. **Chase Safety** - Go/no-go decision and routes

### Recommended Workflow
1. **Monitor** - Watch Radar tab, look for developing storms
2. **Analyze** - Tap promising storm, check Analysis tab
3. **Assess** - Review Week 3 predictions and safety score
4. **Decide** - Use Go/No-Go score and safe havens
5. **Chase** - Follow route, monitor lightning, update database
6. **Learn** - Export data and review lessons afterward

## 💡 Pro Tips

✅ **Enable GPS** - Settings → allows personalized distances and ETAs

✅ **Chase Mode** - Settings → Chase Mode keeps screen on during the day

✅ **Data Saver** - Settings → Data Saver reduces refresh rate for weak signal

✅ **Favorites** - Settings → Save viewing spots for quick navigation

✅ **Dark Mode** - Already enabled by default (easy on eyes at night)

✅ **Glance Mode** - Menu → Glance shows giant-type status from across room

✅ **Tornado History** - Menu → Layers → Shows historical tornado tracks (1950+)

✅ **Video Export** - Snapshot button (📷) → Shares current radar view

## ⚠️ Safety Reminders

1. **AI Analysis is UNOFFICIAL** - Always follow NWS warnings
2. **Verify with Radar** - Don't rely only on scores

4. **Check Lightning** - Monitor proximity alerts constantly
5. **Have Escape Route** - Never drive into rain core
